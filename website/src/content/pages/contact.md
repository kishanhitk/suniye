title: "Contact"
description: "How to reach the Suniye maintainer: report a bug on GitHub, send a security report privately, or use the in-app problem reporter."
---

Suniye is maintained by one person, and every channel below reaches them directly. Pick the one that fits; there is no support team and no ticket queue, so replies usually take a few days rather than minutes.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Open an issue on GitHub: [github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/issues](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/issues). Include your macOS version, the Mac model, which speech model you had selected, and what you expected to happen. Issues are public, so leave out anything you would not want on the internet.

The app also has a built-in reporter — **Report a Problem…** in Suniye's menu bar menu — which sends your description, the app and macOS version, the selected speech model, and optionally your email so the maintainer can follow up. Diagnostic logs are opt-in and you can review them before sending; they exclude audio, transcripts, clipboard contents, and API keys.

## Report a security vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for the repository: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/security/advisories/new). You will get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and a triage decision within 7 days, as set out in the project's [security policy](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Questions, privacy, press

Anything that is not a bug — privacy questions, press, or general feedback — also goes through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/issues). If it must stay private, use the in-app reporter above with your email filled in; it reaches the maintainer without going through a public issue.

## Source and releases

- Source code: [github.com/kishanhitk/suniye](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye)
- Releases and release notes: [github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/releases](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/releases)

## No email, phone, or postal address

Suniye is not a company and has no office; it publishes no email address, phone line, or postal address. GitHub and the in-app reporter are the channels.
