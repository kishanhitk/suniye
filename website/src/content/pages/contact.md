Suniye is maintained by one person, [Kishan Kumar](https://kishans.in/), and every channel below reaches him directly. Pick the one that fits; there is no support team and no ticket queue, so replies usually take a few days rather than minutes.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Open an issue on GitHub: [github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/issues](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/issues). Include your macOS version, the Mac model, which speech model you had selected, and what you expected to happen. Issues are public, so leave out anything you would not want on the internet.

The app also has a built-in reporter — **Report a Problem…** in Suniye's menu bar menu — which sends your description, the app and macOS version, the selected speech model, and optionally your email so the maintainer can follow up. Diagnostic logs are opt-in and you can review them before sending; they exclude audio, transcripts, clipboard contents, and API keys.

## Report a security vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for the repository: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/security/advisories/new). You will get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and a triage decision within 7 days, as set out in the project's [security policy](https://github.com/kishanhitk/suniye/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
