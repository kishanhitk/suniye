"Free" means different things across this list. Some of these apps are free with no catch. Others have a genuinely useful free tier sitting in front of a paid one. Both are legitimate — but worth knowing which is which before you pick one.

## Free with no tier above it

**Suniye** — every feature, every one of its eleven local speech models, and its optional AI cleanup step are free with no upgrade prompt anywhere, because there's no paid tier to upsell into. Open source (MIT), macOS only. [Download it →](/)

**Handy** — MIT licensed, zero cloud component, no account, no word limits. Runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux, which makes it the broadest-reach option on this list if you're not exclusively on a Mac.

**FluidVoice** — free and GPLv3 licensed, with an optional local AI cleanup model (Fluid-1) included at no cost. macOS only, requires macOS 15 or later.

**macOS's built-in Dictation** — already on your Mac. Recent macOS versions run it entirely on-device, with no internet required and nothing to install. It's fixed — one model, no cleanup pass, no customization — but it costs nothing and needs no setup.

## Free tier, paid ceiling above it

**Superwhisper** — free tier covers unlimited use of its smaller local models. Larger models and extra features require Pro, at $8.49/month or $84.99/year.

**Wispr Flow** — free tier covers 2,000 words a week on Mac or Windows (1,000 on iPhone). Past that, Pro is $15/month. Unlike the others here, Wispr Flow's dictation runs in the cloud even on the free tier — there's no local-processing option at any price.

## How to actually choose

If you want something that's free indefinitely and never asks you to upgrade, that's Suniye, Handy, FluidVoice, or macOS's own Dictation — pick based on platform reach (Handy covers the most) and whether you want an AI cleanup pass (Suniye and FluidVoice both offer one; Handy and Apple's built-in tool don't).

If you're fine hitting a ceiling eventually and paying past it, Superwhisper's free tier is a reasonable way to try local dictation with room to grow. Wispr Flow's free tier is really a trial of a cloud product — worth knowing going in, since the tier structure is about usage volume, not about giving you the local option for less.
